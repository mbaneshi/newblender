# Inventory B — Free real files, assets and trainings per stage

- **Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Index:** [03 — Inventory](03-inventory.md)
- **Method:** a web research agent read download.blender.org listings and the per-lesson "Free" badges in studio.blender.org HTML.
  - **V** = seen as free on a primary page or public download listing (files not downloaded, contents not inspected).
  - **U** = unverified.
- **Key fact:** Blender Studio paywalls item by item. A film being CC-BY does **not** make its files free to download.

## Verified facts
- **Demo files:** free, no account needed. Mostly CC-BY or CC0; some CC-BY-SA; a few state no licence (most EEVEE/sculpt/physics demos, Lone Monk, the 2.79–2.81 splashes).
- **Splash scenes:** downloadable for 2.90, 2.91, 3.1, 3.3–3.6, 4.0–4.3, 4.5, 5.1, 5.2 (4.4 and 5.0 missing).
  - From Studio productions: 3.0 Sprite Fright, 3.4 Charge, 3.6 Pet Projects, 4.2 Gold, 4.3 House of Chores, 4.5 DOGWALK, 5.1 Singularity.
  - From the community: 4.0 Gaku, 4.1 Lynxsdesign, 5.2 Panthera Spelaea.
- **Free asset bundles:** Human Base Meshes (CC0), Ellie Pose Library (CC-BY), Cube Diorama (CC0), plus `animal_base_meshes` in the asset-bundles repo.
- **Studio films:** CC-BY 4.0, but production files are paywalled (€11.50/mo).
- **Free open-movie material:**
  - splash .blends
  - the Sprite Fright shot 030_0020_A
  - benchmark scenes (victor, koro)
  - Agent 327 Barbershop and Cosmos Laundromat demos
  - 22 free character rigs
  - the BBB `blender.zip`
  - Tears of Steel masters, raw footage and EXRs (no .blend)
  - **Not found free:** Sintel and Spring production .blends.
- **Fully free Studio courses:** Grease Pencil Fundamentals, Blender 2.8 Fundamentals. **Partly free:** Stylized Character Workflow, GN from Scratch, Scripting for Artists, 3D Printing, Character Animation, Creature Factory 2.
- **Correction to Inventory A:** "Realistic Character Workflow" returns 404 as a Studio course. The real course is "Stylized Character Workflow".
- **Unverified:** official "Blender First Steps", CG Cookie free basics, Blender Guru donut, Rigify manual contents.
